Real Numbers

Quick Revision Notes • 5 min read

Types of Numbers

  • Natural Numbers: 1, 2, 3...
  • Whole Numbers: 0, 1, 2, 3...
  • Integers: -3, -2, -1, 0, 1...
  • Rational Numbers: p/q form
  • Irrational Numbers: √2, π

Real Numbers

Real numbers include all rational and irrational numbers together.

HCF (Highest Common Factor)

Largest number that divides all given numbers.

12 = 2² × 3
18 = 2 × 3²

Common factors = 2 × 3 = 6
HCF = 6

LCM (Least Common Multiple)

Smallest number that is a common multiple.

12 = 2² × 3
18 = 2 × 3²

LCM = 2² × 3² = 36
  • Prime factorization method used
  • HCF → lowest powers
  • LCM → highest powers

Important Formula

LCM × HCF = a × b

Valid for two numbers only.

Important Points

  • ✔ HCF ≤ smallest number
  • ✔ LCM ≥ largest number
  • ✔ Co-prime numbers → HCF = 1
