About Kestrel

Kestrel is a modern learning platform built to help CBSE students revise smarter, practice consistently, and achieve better academic results.

Smart Revision

Every chapter is transformed into concise revision notes that help students understand concepts quickly without unnecessary information.

Practice Tests

Reinforce learning through chapter-wise and subject-wise tests with multiple difficulty levels and instant feedback.

Continuous Progress

Build confidence through regular practice and prepare effectively for school examinations with a structured learning path.

Our Mission

We believe learning should be simple, engaging, and accessible. Kestrel combines clear explanations, focused revision, and meaningful practice to help students learn efficiently rather than spending countless hours memorizing information.
